Illustrative prototype assumptions

The A/B/C bands are product hypotheses, not normative, official or engineering-verified classifications. They describe inputs and do not rate protection.

  • Mass: A < 30; B 30–<100; C 100–250 kg.
  • Speed: A < 60; B 60–<120; C 120–200 km/h.
  • Positive charge: A < 6; B 6–<10; C 10–15 kg.

Higher values are outside the illustrative ranges. Blank and zero charge are separate states. Assumptions are isolated in ASSUMPTIONS; formulas do not depend on these bands.

Formulas & limitations

v = speed / 3.6; E = ½mv². a = length + 2 × offset; b = width + 2 × offset; c = height + top offset. Area = ab + 2ac + 2bc. Fabric mass = area × input mass per area.

This is not an engineering design calculation. It excludes blast loads, fragmentation, attachments and dynamic deflection. It cannot verify any specific net and does not replace specialist engineering review.
